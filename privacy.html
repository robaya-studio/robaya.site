This policy covers the robaya.studio website. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy, which describes the data that app processes — see the links below.

What the website stores

The website remembers the language and the colour theme you choose, in your browser's local storage. That is all: the website uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising, and has no forms that collect personal data.

Hosting and third-party content

The website is delivered by a hosting provider, which processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver pages and protect the website from abuse. The video on the Hwatu page is streamed from Apple's servers, which receive your IP address when the video loads.
