Privacy Policy

Version 1.2.0 — last updated: 13 August 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how I, Arkadiy Tsoy (hereinafter "Developer," "I," or "my"), collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use the mobile game "Hwatu" (hereinafter "Game") on iOS and Android. I comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). By using the Game, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. Information I Collect

When you use the Game, I may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: when you play as a guest, an anonymous account identifier is created for you. If you sign in with Apple or Google, I receive the identifier of that account and, if you choose to share it, your name and email address. You may also set a display name and an avatar inside the Game.
  • Device Information: data about your device, such as device type, operating system, language, unique device identifiers, and crash diagnostics.
  • Usage Data: information about your interaction with the Game, including gameplay statistics, in-app purchases, and ad views.
  • Game Progress: your progress, statistics, and settings, stored in the cloud so they can be restored on your other devices and after reinstalling the Game.
  • Advertising Identifiers: on iOS, the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking; on Android, the advertising ID. Your advertising consent choice is stored on your device.
  • Purchase Information: the fact and status of a purchase or subscription, as reported by the App Store or Google Play. I never receive your payment card details.

3. How I Use Your Data

The data collected is used for the following purposes:

  • To Operate and Improve the Game: to maintain and enhance the functionality of the Game.
  • To Maintain Your Account: to create your account, keep your progress, and restore it on a new device.
  • To Process In-App Purchases: to process purchases of virtual items and to verify the status of a subscription.
  • To Serve Advertisements: to display ads. Ads are personalized only if you have given your consent.
  • To Provide Online Features: leaderboards, tournaments, and multiplayer, where your display name and avatar are shown to other players.
  • To Keep the Game Safe: to prevent abuse and cheating and to diagnose crashes and errors.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

I process your data based on the following legal grounds:

  • Contract Necessity: to run the Game, maintain your account and cloud save, and process purchases.
  • Your Consent: for personalized advertising and, on iOS, for tracking. You may withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Legitimate Interests: to keep the Game secure, prevent abuse, diagnose crashes, and improve the Game.

5. Data Subject Rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access: request information about the data I have collected about you.
  • Rectification: correct any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: request deletion of your data, where possible.
  • Restriction: restrict the processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Objection: object to data processing based on legitimate interests or for advertising purposes.
  • Data Portability: request transfer of your data to another data controller in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: change your advertising choices at any time in the Game under Settings → Agreements → Privacy settings. On iOS you can also revoke tracking permission in the system settings, under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

To exercise these rights, please contact me at robaya.studio@gmail.com.

6. California Consumer Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

Under CCPA/CPRA, you have the right to:

  • Access Request: request information about the personal data I have collected about you, including the sources of data collection and the parties with whom the data has been shared.
  • Deletion Request: request deletion of your personal data.
  • Correction Request: request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing: opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal data.

I do not sell your personal data for money. Showing personalized ads may qualify as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under CPRA. You can opt out at any time under Settings → Agreements → Privacy settings in the Game, after which ads are no longer based on your interests.

To exercise these rights, please contact me at robaya.studio@gmail.com.

7. Data Sharing

I do not sell your personal information to third parties. Data is shared only with the service providers that make the Game work, and where required by law:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, Cloud Functions): accounts, cloud save, usage statistics, crash reports, and verification of purchases.
  • Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, and Google Play Games: signing in to your account and leaderboards.
  • AppLovin MAX and the advertising networks it mediates: serving ads in the Game.
  • Google User Messaging Platform: showing the consent form and storing your advertising choice.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: processing purchases and subscriptions.
  • Legal Requirements: I may disclose your information if required by law.

These providers process data on my behalf, or as independent controllers under their own privacy policies, and are obliged to protect your data.

8. Advertising and Consent

The Game shows ads to players without an active premium subscription. Advertising is mediated by AppLovin MAX. On first launch in the EEA, the UK, and other regions where this is required, you are shown a consent form from a Google-certified consent management platform, where you decide whether your data may be used for personalized advertising. On iOS you are additionally asked for permission to track; if you decline, the advertising identifier is not used. If you decline personalized advertising, ads are still shown, but they are not based on your interests. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Agreements → Privacy settings. An active premium subscription removes interstitial ads; rewarded ads remain available if you choose to watch them.

9. Cloud Save and Online Features

Your progress is stored in the cloud (Google Firebase) under your account, so it can be restored on another device and after reinstalling the Game. If you play as a guest, your progress is tied to an anonymous account on that device — sign in with Apple or Google to keep it. In online modes, leaderboards, and tournaments, your display name and avatar are visible to other players, so please do not use personal data in your display name.

Earlier versions of the Game for iOS stored progress in Apple iCloud. When you update to the current version, that data is transferred once into the cloud account described above.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Account and progress data is kept for as long as your account exists. Usage statistics and crash reports are kept according to the standard retention periods of the services listed in section 7. To delete your account and the data associated with it, write to robaya.studio@gmail.com — such requests are processed within 30 days. Deleting the Game from your device does not by itself delete data stored in the cloud.

11. International Data Transfers

Data is processed on the servers of Google and the other providers listed in section 7, which may be located outside your country, including in the United States. Such transfers are covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or by equivalent safeguards offered by those providers.

12. Data Storage and Security

I implement reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access and loss. However, no method of data transmission over the internet can guarantee absolute security.

13. Children's Privacy

The Game is not intended for children under the age of 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If I become aware that I have collected such information, I will take steps to delete it.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted in the Game or on my website. Continued use of the Game after changes signifies your acceptance of the revised policy.

15. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact me at robaya.studio@gmail.com.
