About the game

A battle of flowers: twelve months in one deck

Hwatu — literally “battle of flowers” — is played with a deck of 48 cards: twelve months of four cards each, every month with its own plant. Take cards from the table by matching months, collect combinations and outscore your opponents.

  • Yagi, yonko and kanceri — the combinations you know from the table
  • Pregnant tens and twenties: if nobody collects them, they are worth double next game, then triple
  • House rules you switch on and off: hadai doesn't pay, postman doesn't pay, pohato on three yagi and more
  • Choose the stake and the number of players at the table — from two to five
