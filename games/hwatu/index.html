About the game

A battle of flowers: twelve months in one deck

Hwatu means “battle of flowers”. The deck holds 48 cards — twelve months, four cards each, and every month has its own flower: keklik, myadya, sagura and on through the year. Match the months, build combinations, keep an eye on your rivals — and take the winnings.

  • Yagi, yonko and kanceri — just like at a real table
  • Pregnant tens and twenties: if nobody takes them, they're worth double next game, then triple — and the tension builds
  • Play by your house rules: hadai doesn't pay, postman doesn't pay, pohato on three yagi and more — switch them on in the settings
  • Two to five players, and the stake is up to you
