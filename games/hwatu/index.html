About Hwatu

Experience the rich tradition of Korean card gaming with Hwatu! This classic card game, whose name translates to "Battle of Flowers," has been played for centuries and remains a beloved pastime in Korea. Play offline against bots, gather friends in a private online room, or take part in tournaments — with authentic cards, the familiar rules of go-stop, and your progress saved to the cloud.
