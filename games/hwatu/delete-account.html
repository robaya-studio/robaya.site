Deleting your Hwatu account and data

Hwatu — ROBAYA Studio

You can delete your Hwatu account and all data linked to it at any time. Deletion is immediate and applies on every device where you play.

In the game

  • Open Hwatu and go to Profile.
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap “Delete account” (if you play as a guest, the item is called “Delete my data”).
  • Confirm. The account and its data are deleted at once, on every device.

Without the game

If you no longer have the game, write to robaya.studio@gmail.com and tell us your display name in the game and, if you can, the platform (Android or iOS) and roughly when you last played, so that we can find your account. Requests are processed within 30 days.

robaya.studio@gmail.com

What is deleted

  • Your account and progress: level, achievements, statistics and settings.
  • Coins and items, whether earned or bought.
  • Game history, online rating and tournament entries.
  • Messages sent to support and records of your acceptance of the agreements.
  • The link to your Google Play Games or Game Center account. On iOS, progress of the earlier version of the game kept in your iCloud is erased too.

What is kept

  • Records of purchases, so that the same purchase cannot be credited to several accounts. They contain no name or contact details, and their link to the deleted account is removed.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports held by the services named in the Privacy Policy, for their standard periods (usage statistics — 2 months).

Subscriptions

Deleting the account does not cancel an active subscription: cancel it in the Google Play or App Store settings. If you keep it, it passes to the next game account you use with the same Google Play or App Store account.

Deleting the game from your device does not delete your account. An account that is not linked to Google Play Games or Game Center and has not been used for 12 months is deleted automatically, unless it has purchases.
